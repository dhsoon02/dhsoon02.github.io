Research

My research focuses on developing models for reconstructing, animating, and generating dynamic 3D and 4D content, especially for animals and humans. My work combines synthetic data pipelines, parametric animal and human models, and generative methods to turn 2D images and videos into animatable 3D/4D representations. I enjoy building tools and models that expand the creative and scientific possibilities of computer vision and graphics.
